# Email alerts toggle turns back on after reopening Settings

**Labels:** `bug`, `settings`

## Summary
The 'Email alerts' toggle turns back on after navigating away and reopening Settings.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open Settings > Notifications.
2. Toggle 'Email alerts' off.
3. Go back.
4. Reopen Settings.

## Expected Behavior
The 'Email alerts' toggle should stay off.

## Actual Behavior
The toggle is on again.

## Environment
- App Version: v2.4.1
- OS: Android 14
- Device: Pixel 8

## Additional Notes
- Frequency: Happens every time.
