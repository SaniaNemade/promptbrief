# 'Email alerts' toggle reverts to on after leaving Settings

**Labels:** `bug`, `settings`

## Summary
Toggling 'Email alerts' off does not persist. After navigating back and reopening the screen, the toggle reverts to on.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open Settings > Notifications.
2. Toggle 'Email alerts' off.
3. Navigate back.
4. Reopen Settings and return to Notifications.

## Expected Behavior
The 'Email alerts' toggle should remain off.

## Actual Behavior
The 'Email alerts' toggle reverts to on.

## Environment
- OS: Android 14
- App Version: v2.4.1
- Device: Pixel 8

## Additional Notes
Assumptions:
- Assumed the user navigated back to the Notifications screen after reopening Settings to observe the toggle state.
